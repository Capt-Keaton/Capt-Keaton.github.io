About Me

Grounded in curiosity,
built for altitude.

I'm Keaton — drawn to industries where precision and ambition meet: aviation, energy, technology, and the built world. I move between boardroom and runway thinking with the same discipline pilots bring to a pre-flight checklist: prepare thoroughly, respect the systems, and commit fully once cleared for takeoff.

Outside of work, I chase adrenaline the way I chase ideas — behind the wheel of a Formula 1 broadcast, freefalling from altitude, or descending into open water. I also host conversations on wealth, health and family, because I believe a good life is built the same way a good flight plan is: intentionally, and one leg at a time.
